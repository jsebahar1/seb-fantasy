# College Football Team Logo Attribution

The 138 PNG team badges in this directory were retrieved from the official
[TheSportsDB API](https://www.thesportsdb.com/documentation) on September 17,
2026. The files are stored locally and used unmodified.

TheSportsDB is the source of the artwork files. Its terms permit API content to
be used in development projects and require trademarked sports logos to remain
unaltered. See the [TheSportsDB terms of use](https://www.thesportsdb.com/docs_terms_of_use.php).

All university names, logos, and related marks remain the property of their
respective owners. Their inclusion identifies the teams represented in the
rankings and does not imply endorsement or affiliation.

The pooled `FCS` model entry stands in for many programs rather than one team,
so instead of a school badge it uses the official NCAA Division I FCS
championship shield (`fcs.png`), retrieved September 17, 2026. That mark is the
property of the NCAA; its inclusion identifies the FCS bucket in the rankings
and does not imply endorsement or affiliation.
