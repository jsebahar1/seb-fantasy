# NFL Team Logo Attribution

The 32 PNG team badges in this directory were retrieved from the official
[TheSportsDB API](https://www.thesportsdb.com/documentation) on September 17,
2026 — the same source used for the college football badges in
`public/team-logos/cfb/`. The files are stored locally and used unmodified.

TheSportsDB is the source of the artwork files. Its terms permit API content to
be used in development projects and require trademarked sports logos to remain
unaltered. See the [TheSportsDB terms of use](https://www.thesportsdb.com/docs_terms_of_use.php).

All team names, logos, and related marks remain the property of their
respective owners (the NFL and its member clubs). Their inclusion identifies
the teams represented in the rankings and does not imply endorsement or
affiliation.
